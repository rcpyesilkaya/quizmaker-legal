Frequently asked questions

Where is my data?

On your device. Nothing is uploaded unless you turn on cloud AI in Settings → Privacy & AI.

"On-device AI isn't available"

It needs a device with Apple Intelligence turned on. Otherwise you can allow cloud AI, write your own questions, or extract term–definition cards without AI.

Which files are supported?

PDF, Word (.docx), PowerPoint (.pptx), text, photos and scans, and audio recordings. Save old .doc/.ppt files as .docx/.pptx.

How does the study streak work?

A day counts when you finish a quiz or a flashcard session. One rest day per week keeps your streak going. Reminders are optional and can be changed in Settings.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Quiz Maker and tap Cancel Subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the Pro screen or Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple ID you used to buy.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete All Data.
